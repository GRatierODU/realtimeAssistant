Anthropic launches Claude Science - AI workbench for researchers

  Impact:          [HIGH] -> [RESEARCH] Scientific research / Pharma vertical
  PHIA confidence: High (primary + TechCrunch corroboration)
  Probability:     Almost certain (beta live today)

Anthropic introduced Claude Science, a macOS/Linux workbench integrating 60+
scientific databases, MCP connectors, and HPC clusters (SSH/Modal). Produces
auditable research artifacts. This is NOT a new model - it runs standard Claude
including Opus 4.8. Beta on Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise plans.

  Funding: Up to 50 research projects receive up to $30,000 in credits.
           Applications close July 15, 2026.

Why it matters:
  Signals Anthropic's vertical strategy (like Claude Code for software).
  Directly competes with OpenAI's science benchmarking push on the same day.

Sources:
  - Anthropic (primary): https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-science-ai-workbench

  Impact:          [EMERGING] -> [RESEARCH] AI-for-science evaluation
  PHIA confidence: High (primary source + public Hugging Face dataset)

EU receives 76 bids for 20 billion euro AI gigafactory program

  Impact:          [CRITICAL] -> [GLOBAL] EU tech sovereignty / AI infrastructure
  PHIA confidence: High (Reuters wire, official EU press conference)
  Probability:     Highly likely (official EU announcement)

Microsoft Research unveils Memora - agent memory architecture

  Impact:          [EMERGING] -> [RESEARCH] AI agents / Infrastructure
  PHIA confidence: High (peer-reviewed ICML 2026, open-source code)
  Probability:     Almost certain (published research; not yet in products)

Microsoft Research released Memora, a harmonic memory framework that decouples
storage from retrieval using abstractions and cue anchors.

  LoCoMo (600-turn dialogues):     86.3% accuracy
  LongMemEval (115K-token context): 87.4% accuracy
  Token reduction:                  up to 98% vs. full-context inference

Why it matters:
  Addresses a core bottleneck for long-running AI agents. Code is on GitHub but
  not yet integrated into Copilot products.

Sources:
  - Microsoft Research (primary): https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/memora-a-harmonic-memory-representation-balancing-abstraction-and-specificity/
  - InfoWorld: https://www.infoworld.com/article/4191031/microsoft-unveils-memora-to-tackle-ai-agents-memory-problem.html

US lawmakers propose ban on selling AI chatbot health data

  Impact:          [MODERATE] -> [POLICY] US privacy regulation
  PHIA confidence: Moderate (bill announced, not yet introduced or passed)
  Probability:     Realistic possibility (uncertain Congressional path)

Sen. Elizabeth Warren (D-MA) and Rep. Mary Gay Scanlon (D-PA) plan to introduce
an updated Health and Location Data Protection Act. It would ban sale of health
and location data - including information entered into AI chatbots - to data
brokers. Would empower FTC enforcement with $1 billion over 10 years.

Note: Legislative proposal from Democratic lawmakers. Reported factually per PHIA
independence standard. Not yet law.

Why it matters:
  First major federal proposal specifically targeting AI health data
  monetization as users upload medical records to ChatGPT, Claude, and similar
  tools.

Sources:
  - The Verge (June 29): https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/959033/health-location-data-protection-act-ai-warren-scanlon
